# Hawking Radiation

Hawking radiation is the thermal emission predicted for black holes by quantum
field theory on a curved background. It makes black holes behave like black
bodies with a temperature set by their surface gravity.

## Surface gravity and temperature

The surface gravity of a static black hole is
$\kappa=\lim_{r\to r_+}\tfrac{c^2}{2}f'(r)$. For [[Schwarzschild Metric]] this
gives $\kappa=c^4/(4GM)$, and Hawking's calculation yields

$$T_H = \frac{\hbar\kappa}{2\pi k_B c} = \frac{\hbar c^3}{8\pi G M k_B}.$$

A solar-mass hole has $T_H\approx6\times10^{-8}$ K, far colder than the CMB, so
it currently absorbs more than it emits.

## Power, lifetime, and entropy

Neglecting greybody factors, the radiated power and evaporation time are

$$P = \frac{\hbar c^6}{15360\pi G^2 M^2}, \qquad
\tau = \frac{5120\pi G^2 M_0^3}{\hbar c^4}.$$

For a solar mass, $\tau\sim2\times10^{67}$ yr, about $10^{57}$ times the age of
the Universe. The Bekenstein–Hawking entropy is

$$S_{\rm BH} = \frac{k_B c^3 A}{4G\hbar} = \frac{4\pi k_B G M^2}{\hbar c}.$$

## Thermodynamics and the information paradox

The four laws of black-hole mechanics hold with $\kappa$ as temperature and $A$
as entropy; the first law is $dM=(\kappa/8\pi G)dA+\Omega_H dJ+\Phi dQ$ (see
[[Kerr Metric]] and [[Kerr-Newman Metric]]). Because the radiation is thermal,
a fully evaporated hole appears to destroy information, motivating the
information paradox and proposed resolutions such as remnants, correlations
(Page curve), holography, and firewalls. The deep connection to quantum
spacetime is treated in [[Quantum Gravity]], and the time-reversed analog in
[[White Holes]].
