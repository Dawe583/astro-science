# Quantum Gravity

Quantum gravity is the attempt to describe spacetime and gravity quantum
mechanically. No complete theory is experimentally confirmed, but the
motivations and candidate frameworks are well mapped.

## Why it is needed

General relativity makes geometry dynamical, while quantum field theory assumes
a fixed background. Matter QFT on a curved background is predictive and gives
Hawking radiation ([[Hawking Radiation]]), but at the Planck scale or during
black-hole evaporation a quantum description of spacetime itself is required.

## Planck scales

Combining $G$, $\hbar$, and $c$ gives

$$\ell_P=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar G}{c^3}}, \quad t_P=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar G}{c^5}}, \quad
m_P=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar c}{G}}, \quad T_P=\frac{m_Pc^2}{k_B}.$$

The Schwarzschild radius and the Compton wavelength cross near $m\approx m_P/\sqrt2$,
which is the order-of-magnitude motivation for the Planck scale.

## Research result (2026-10-28)

**Observational constraints.** LQG predicts discrete area spectra
($A_{\min} \approx 5.17\,\ell_P^2$ for $j=1/2$). String theory predicts extra
dimensions and supersymmetry. Current observations constrain but do not confirm
either: EHT shadow (M87*) deviation < 10%, GW ringdown (GW150914) QNM
deviation < 20%, GRB time-of-flight $E_{QG} > 10^{17}$ GeV. The LHC reaches
$10^{-15}$ of the Planck energy; cosmic rays reach $10^{-8}$. No terrestrial
or astrophysical experiment approaches the Planck scale.

## Approaches

Gravitational effective field theory is predictive order by order below its
cutoff but does not specify a UV completion. Loop quantum gravity, string theory,
asymptotic safety, and causal dynamical triangulations are distinct candidate
programs, each with open questions. The Bekenstein–Hawking entropy
$S_{\rm BH}=k_BA/4\ell_P^2$ is a key target for any microscopic theory. In loop
quantum gravity the singularity resolves into a bounce, connecting to
[[White Holes]]. Proposed Lorentz-violating photon dispersion,
$E^2\simeq p^2c^2[1+\eta(E/E_{QG})^n]$, is constrained by GRB 090510
($E_{QG,1}/E_P>1.19$) and by GW170817/GRB 170817A. Wormhole exotic matter in
[[Wormholes]] also motivates quantum-gravity effects.
