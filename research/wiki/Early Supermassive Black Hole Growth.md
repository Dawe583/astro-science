# Early Supermassive Black Hole Growth

How supermassive black holes reached billions of solar masses within the first
billion years is an open problem. Observations of high-redshift quasars and
AGN set the growth budget; accretion physics supplies the clock.

## The growth equation

The Eddington luminosity for fully ionized hydrogen is
$L_{\rm Edd}=4\pi GMm_pc/\sigma_T\approx1.26\times10^{38}(M/M_\odot)$ erg/s.
With radiative efficiency $\epsilon$, Eddington ratio
$\lambda=L/L_{\rm Edd}$, and duty fraction $f_{\rm duty}$, the retained mass
grows as

$$\frac{dM}{dt}=\frac{1-\epsilon}{\epsilon}f_{\rm duty}\lambda\frac{4\pi Gm_p}{\sigma_T c}M,$$

so $M=M_0\exp[(t-t_0)/t_{\rm ef}]$ with e-folding time

$$t_{\rm ef}=\frac{\epsilon}{1-\epsilon}\frac{\sigma_T c}{4\pi Gm_p}\frac{1}{f_{\rm duty}\lambda}.$$

For $\epsilon=0.1$ and continuous Eddington-limited accretion, $t_{\rm ef}\approx50$ Myr.

## Observational ledger

High-redshift systems include the quasar J0313$-$1806 at $z=7.64$
($M_{\rm BH}=(1.6\pm0.4)\times10^9M_\odot$), CEERS_1019 at $z=8.68$
($\log_{10}M_{\rm BH}=6.95\pm0.37$), GN-z11 at $z=10.6$, and the X-ray source
UHZ1 at $z\approx10.1$. Abell 2744-QSO1 at $z=7.04$ is notable for resolved gas
kinematics. Each measurement has distinct method systematics; none establishes
a unique seed channel.

## Seed channels

Population III remnants, direct-collapse heavy seeds, nuclear-cluster pathways,
and primordial black holes remain viable. Earlier formation, more massive
seeds, higher duty cycles, or mergers can compensate for one another, so a
final mass and epoch constrain the integrated budget, not the seed alone. The
accretion physics connects to [[Black Hole Accretion]] and the remnant masses
to [[Gravitational Wave Analysis]]; primordial seeds connect to [[Quantum Gravity]].
