# Kerr-Newman Metric

The Kerr–Newman metric is the most general stationary, axisymmetric,
asymptotically flat solution of the Einstein–Maxwell equations: a black hole
with mass $M$, spin $a=J/M$, and charge $Q$.

## Metric and horizons

In Boyer–Lindquist coordinates,

$$\Delta = r^2 - 2GMr + a^2 + GQ^2, \qquad \Sigma = r^2 + a^2\cos^2\theta,$$

and the electromagnetic potential is
$A_\mu dx^\mu = -(GQr/\Sigma)dt + (GQra\sin^2\theta/\Sigma)d\phi$. Horizons
satisfy $\Delta=0$:

$$r_\pm = GM \pm \sqrt{G^2M^2 - a^2 - GQ^2},$$

with extremality $G^2M^2=a^2+GQ^2$ and horizon area $A=4\pi(r_+^2+a^2)$. The
ergosphere boundary is $r_{\rm ergo}(\theta)=GM+\sqrt{G^2M^2-a^2\cos^2\theta-GQ^2}$.

## Thermodynamics and invariants

Surface gravity, temperature, and horizon angular velocity are

$$\kappa = \frac{r_+-r_-}{2(r_+^2+a^2)}, \qquad
T_H = \frac{\kappa}{2\pi}, \qquad
\Omega_H = \frac{a}{r_+^2+a^2}.$$

The first law and Smarr formula are

$$dM = \frac{\kappa}{8\pi G}dA + \Omega_H dJ + \Phi_+ dQ, \qquad
M = 2T_H S + 2\Omega_H J + \Phi_+ Q.$$

The black hole has gyromagnetic ratio $g=2$, like a Dirac particle, and obeys
$G^2M^2 \ge a^2 + GQ^2$ (cosmic censorship).

## Hidden symmetries and mass

Geodesics are integrable because of a rank-2 Killing tensor and the Carter
constant; see [[Geodesic Equations]]. Conserved charges can be computed as ADM,
Komar, or Wald (Noether) surface integrals, all agreeing: $M_{\rm ADM}=M$,
$J_{\rm ADM}=Ma$, $Q_{\rm ADM}=Q$. The Christodoulou–Ruffini mass formula
splits the mass into irreducible, Coulomb, and rotational parts. The uncharged
limit is [[Kerr Metric]]; setting $a=0$ gives [[Reissner-Nordström Metric]].
