# Raychaudhuri Equation

The Raychaudhuri equation describes how a congruence of geodesics expands,
shears, and rotates. It is the geometric heart of the focusing theorem and the
singularity theorems.

## Kinematics

For a timelike congruence with $u^\mu u_\mu=-1$ and projection tensor
$h_{\mu\nu}=g_{\mu\nu}+u_\mu u_\nu$, the derivative decomposes as

$$\nabla_\nu u_\mu = \frac13\theta h_{\mu\nu}+\sigma_{\mu\nu}+\omega_{\mu\nu}-u_\mu a_\nu,$$

where $\theta=\nabla_\mu u^\mu$ is the expansion, $\sigma_{\mu\nu}$ the shear,
$\omega_{\mu\nu}$ the vorticity, and $a_\mu$ the acceleration (zero for
geodesics). The null case uses a 2-screen projection with an auxiliary null
vector $\ell^\mu$ satisfying $k^\mu\ell_\mu=-1$.

## The equations

For a timelike geodesic congruence,

$$\frac{d\theta}{d\tau} = -\frac13\theta^2-\sigma_{\mu\nu}\sigma^{\mu\nu}+\omega_{\mu\nu}\omega^{\mu\nu}-R_{\mu\nu}u^\mu u^\nu,$$

and for an affinely parametrized null congruence,

$$\frac{d\theta}{d\lambda} = -\frac12\theta^2-\sigma_{\mu\nu}\sigma^{\mu\nu}+\omega_{\mu\nu}\omega^{\mu\nu}-R_{\mu\nu}k^\mu k^\nu.$$

Expansion and shear focus the congruence; vorticity defocuses it. If the
congruence is hypersurface-orthogonal ($\omega=0$), vorticity stays zero.

## Focusing and singularity theorems

With the strong energy condition ($R_{\mu\nu}u^\mu u^\nu\ge0$) or the null
energy condition ($R_{\mu\nu}k^\mu k^\nu\ge0$), initially converging congruences
focus to a caustic in finite affine parameter. This underlies the Penrose,
Hawking, and Hawking–Penrose singularity theorems and Hawking's area theorem.
It also governs gravitational lensing, where $d\ln A/d\lambda=\theta$, and
black-hole thermodynamics, where equilibrium requires $\theta=0$. The
equations are built from [[Christoffel Symbols]] and the [[Geodesic Equations]],
and they connect to the NEC-violating matter of [[White Holes]] and to
accretion flows in [[Black Hole Accretion]].
